  • Python 3 available

05

Your assignment

Employee Anniversary Recognition Feed

TSP requires a simple outbound integration that sends employees reaching a relevant service anniversary to a fictional external provider called Employee Recognition Provider.

This is a framework demonstration. Only fictional employee data may be used. No real employee or production data may be accessed.

06

When the agent asks for the Functional Specification

Copy the complete Functional Specification and provide it to the agent.

Includes the test data, open items and functional clarification rule.

07

What if the agent asks you a question?

FUNCTIONAL QUESTION

“What anniversary rule should apply?”
“What is the minimum number of years?”

For this demo, you are also acting as the Functional Owner.

Invent a reasonable business answer and provide it to the agent.

Do not ask the facilitator for the answer.

TECHNICAL QUESTION

“Should this be implemented in ABAP or CPI?”
“Where should the CSV be created?”
“What integration pattern should be used?”

Do not invent this as a business requirement.

Ask the agent to resolve it through the technical engineering process.

Suggested response

This is a technical design decision. Please evaluate it through the framework and recommend the appropriate solution.
08

Three rules

01

Follow the agent.

Do not try to navigate the framework manually.

02

Ask before guessing.

If you don't know what the framework expects next, ask:

What should I do next?
03

Don't clean up the result.

When the exercise finishes, do not delete, rename or reorganize generated framework artifacts.

We want to review the project exactly as it finished.

Never commit passwords, private keys, tokens, productive employee data or other secrets.

09

When are you done?

Do not decide manually that the exercise is complete.

Continue until the framework itself indicates that the authorized development scope is complete.

No productive deployment is required.
